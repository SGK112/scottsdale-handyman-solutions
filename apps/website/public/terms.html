Terms of Service

Scottsdale Handyman Solutions LLC · Licensed, Bonded & Insured · Serving Scottsdale, Paradise Valley & Fountain Hills

These terms apply to all work performed by Scottsdale Handyman Solutions LLC (“we”, “us”) for our customers (“you”), including one-time service calls, package services, monthly maintenance plans, and emergency call-outs. Booking a service or enrolling in a plan means you accept these terms.

1. Estimates & Scope of Work

  • Estimates are free and valid for 30 days from the date issued.
  • An estimate covers only the work described in it. Work discovered after we open a wall, cabinet, or fixture — or any change you request on site — is quoted separately and must be approved by you before we proceed.
  • Package prices assume normal residential conditions and reasonable access to the work area.

2. Pricing & Payment

  • Payment is due on completion of the work unless agreed otherwise in writing.
  • Projects quoted above $1,000 may require a deposit before scheduling; the balance is due on completion.
  • We accept major credit cards, debit, check, and ACH transfer.
  • Materials are billed at cost unless the package you selected states materials are included.
  • Invoices unpaid after 30 days may accrue a 1.5% monthly late charge.

3. Monthly Maintenance Plans

Maintenance plans are recurring subscriptions billed monthly in advance to the payment method on file.

  • Plans renew automatically each month until cancelled.
  • Cancel any time with 30 days’ notice — no long-term contract and no cancellation penalty.
  • Plan visits are scheduled by appointment. Unused visits do not roll over to the following month.
  • The plan discount applies to labor on additional services and does not apply to materials or third-party costs.
  • Plan members receive priority scheduling ahead of non-member requests.
Rental & investment properties. Maintenance plans are available per property. If you own multiple units, each address is enrolled separately, and we can send visit reports to you rather than to the occupant.

4. Emergency Service

  • Emergency service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays.
  • Emergency service is for urgent conditions only — active leaks, electrical safety hazards, and security issues such as a door or window that will not secure.
  • The emergency call-out rate is flat and stated at the time of booking. We do not add overtime, weekend, or holiday surcharges on top of the emergency rate.
  • Parts and materials needed to complete an emergency repair are billed in addition to the call-out.
  • Where a permanent repair requires ordered parts, we will make the property safe and return to complete the work.

5. Scheduling, Cancellation & Access

  • Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule a booked appointment.
  • If our technician arrives and cannot access the property, a trip charge may apply.
  • You are responsible for providing safe, lawful access to the work area and for clearing valuables and obstructions from it.
  • Tenant-occupied properties: if you are the owner or property manager booking work at a property occupied by someone else, you confirm you are authorized to approve the work and to arrange entry, and that the occupant will be given any notice their lease or Arizona law requires.

7. Licensing, Insurance & Permits

  • We are licensed, bonded, and insured in the State of Arizona. Proof is available on request.

We stand behind our work with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If something is not right, tell us within 7 days of completion and we will return to correct it at no additional labor charge. Our goal is always to fix the problem first. Any dispute not resolved directly will be governed by the laws of the State of Arizona, with venue in Maricopa County.

We are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, including lost rent or lost business income. Nothing here limits liability for personal injury caused by our negligence.
