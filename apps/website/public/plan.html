Questions

The things people ask.

What happens in an emergency?

We answer 24/7 — active leaks, electrical safety problems, and doors or windows that will not secure. Emergency call-out is $149 flat, and we never add overtime, weekend or holiday charges on top. Plan members get priority dispatch and 15% off the additional work.

Is there a contract?

No. The plan renews monthly and you can cancel any time with 30 days' notice. There is no cancellation penalty.

What if the property needs work beyond the checklist?

We quote it before we start, and plan members get 15% off the labor. Nothing extra happens without your say-so. Jobs over $1,000 take a deposit with the balance due on completion.

Do you need my tenant to be home?

Usually not, if we can arrange access. We coordinate with your tenant directly and give whatever notice their lease and Arizona law require. You stay the one who authorizes the work.

Are you licensed and insured?

Licensed, bonded and insured in the State of Arizona. Proof is available on request. Our workmanship warranty is 90 days on service calls, and 1–2 years on larger projects.

What if I have several properties?

Each address is enrolled on its own plan and keeps its own service record, so you can see them separately. Tell us how many on the survey and we will walk them together.
